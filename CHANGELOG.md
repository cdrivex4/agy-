- Upstream compatibility tracking against Antigravity CLI v1.2.2.
- Clean-room adapter interfaces in `internal/upstream/agy/`.
- Architecture Decision Records (ADR-0001 through ADR-0007).
- Command registry and policy engine for YOLO mode.
